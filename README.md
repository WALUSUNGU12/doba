# DOBADoba
DOBADoba – Mzuzu's local marketplace & delivery platform. Connecting buyers and sellers in Northern Malawi with easy browsing, secure payments, and reliable last-mile delivery.

## 🌟 About DOBADoba

DOBADoba is a hyper-local e-commerce platform built for **Mzuzu and the Northern Region**. 

Sellers (shops, small businesses, farmers, tailors, etc.) can list their products and services on a simple dashboard. Customers can browse, order, pay securely, and track their items in real-time as our courier service handles delivery.

We solve the biggest challenges in Mzuzu:
- Limited visibility for small shops
- Unreliable or expensive delivery
- Trust and payment fears for online buyers

**Our mission**: Empower local merchants (especially women and youth traders), create jobs for couriers, save customers time and money, and keep the Northern economy strong.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Seller Dashboard** — Easy product listing, order management, and earnings tracking (WhatsApp-friendly onboarding)
- **Customer Marketplace** — Browse goods and services from different Mzuzu merchants
- **Secure Payments** — Airtel Money, TNM Mpamba, Cash on Delivery (COD), and bank options
- **Last-Mile Delivery** — Same-day or next-day delivery by DOBADoba couriers (bicycle/motorcycle fleet)
- **Real-time Tracking** — Live updates via SMS, WhatsApp, and the app
- **Trust System** — Seller ratings, reviews, photo proof of delivery, and DOBADoba Guarantee
- **Mobile-First** — Simple web + Android app (Chichewa/Tumbuka support planned)

## Commission Structure

We take a **seller-friendly 12% commission** only after successful delivery (lower rates for high-volume sellers and launch partners).  
First 3 months: **8%** or free for early merchants.
